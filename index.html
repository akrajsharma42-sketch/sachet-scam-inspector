Scams to recognise

The most common frauds reaching Indian phones today.

Bank or KYC update

“Your account will be blocked” plus a link. The link opens a copy of the bank’s site that steals your login and OTP. Banks do not ask for KYC through SMS links.

Delivery fee

A parcel is “held” and you must pay a small fee. The payment page captures your card or UPI details. Track parcels only in the courier’s own app.

Police, CBI or “digital arrest”

A caller says you are in a case and pushes you to pay or stay on video. No agency arrests people over WhatsApp and takes money.

Prizes, refunds and job tasks

You “won” a draw, are owed a refund, or can earn per day by liking videos. Anything that asks you to pay first is a scam.

Instant loan offers

“Pre-approved” or “final disbursal stage” messages you never applied for. They end with a processing fee and stolen identity documents.

Fake apps and remote access

Links that download an .apk, or callers who ask you to install AnyDesk or TeamViewer. These let a stranger read your OTPs and control your phone.
